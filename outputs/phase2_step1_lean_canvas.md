## Riskiest assumption

The bottleneck is knowledge of QA tools; if the real barrier is visa sponsorship or lack of access to hiring channels, a course will not solve the unemployment problem.

## Demand test

Target: **55 waitlist sign-ups from people in the target market**, fixed by the founder before Validate started. It cannot be changed now.

Build a simple landing page describing the 'AI-Assisted QA Tester' path without selling anything yet. Share it in two specific LinkedIn groups: one for 'International CS Students' and one for 'US Tech Hiring'. Post once per day for 7 days. The signal is one person from outside the founder's network who explicitly asks how to join or requests more details, indicating genuine interest in this specific role.

Only results from real people count. Foundry does not create sign-ups, and simulated or test entries are never counted toward the target.
